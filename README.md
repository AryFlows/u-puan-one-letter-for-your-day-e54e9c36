Entry for the make.fun competition
[One Letter for Your Day](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-one-letter-for-your-day-e54e9c36):
send exactly one letter, A to Z, standing for the word that describes your day.

## The entry

**H** — one character, and nothing else.

The word behind the letter is **hungover**: the day after a night that went
one drink too far — sunglasses indoors, a head full of static, and a greasy
breakfast doing the work of a rescue team.

## Why this letter

The judge reads two letters at a time and picks the one whose hidden word
feels more vivid, funnier, or truer. H points at one word only: no other
H-word describes a day with the same sharp picture. It is specific (a state
you can see and hear), funny in a way that lands at once, and honest — an
ordinary day, reported straight, not performed. The breathy H even sounds
like the groan the word starts with.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes exactly `H` (1 byte, no trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(`/output/entry.txt` by default). Node stdlib only.
